## **4. Proposed Method** 

Our proposed approach is Method 1, in which raw audio is forwarded directly to a multimodal LLM for scam detection without any preprocessing. Methods 2 and 3 serve as comparison baselines using text-based input. In Method 2, audio is first converted to text via the Scribe V1 ASR system and passed to the LLM without correction. In Method 3, the ASR transcript is further reviewed and corrected by a fluent Turkish speaker before being passed to the LLM.
